FAQ

Good questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book directly with us. If you'd like to claim from your insurer, check whether they require a prescription — many don't.

What should I wear to my appointment?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, like shorts or leggings and a T-shirt. We may need to see the area being treated, and we'll always ask for your consent first.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition, but most patients notice a clear improvement within 3 to 5 sessions. We'll give you an honest estimate after your first assessment.

What if I need to cancel?

No problem — just let us know at least 24 hours in advance by phone, email or the link in your confirmation email. Late cancellations may be charged.

Is the clinic accessible?

Yes. We're on the ground floor with step-free access, an accessible restroom and height-adjustable treatment tables.
