How we work

No catalogue, no shortcuts. Just a clear process, and you're involved at every step.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We meet at your home or in the workshop to understand how you live, what you need and what you love.

    Week 1
  2. 02

    Design & drawings

    Sketches, then detailed drawings and wood samples. You approve every dimension and finish before we cut.

    Weeks 2–3
  3. 03

    Making

    Your piece is built by hand in our Lyon workshop, using sustainably sourced French hardwoods and traditional joinery.

    Weeks 4–10
  4. 04

    Delivery & installation

    We deliver and install it ourselves, then explain how to care for it so it ages beautifully.

    Lifetime care
