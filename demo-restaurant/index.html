Our story

Three generations, one kitchen.

Marcel opened the doors in 1987 with twelve tables, a blackboard and a simple promise: cook what the market brings in that morning, and cook it properly.

Today his granddaughter Camille runs the kitchen, but the spirit hasn't changed. Our bread comes from the bakery across the street, our vegetables from three farms within 40 km, and our wine list is a love letter to small producers from Bordeaux and beyond.

  • 40 kmmax. from farm to plate
  • 120+wines by small producers
  • 36seats, plus a summer terrace
